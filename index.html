Our Story

Oreo Delightful Creations began with a simple belief: every celebration deserves something made with care, not just convenience. What started as baking for family and friends grew into a full events and catering brand trusted across Osogbo.

Today, we bring that same warmth to every cake, snack tray and event we touch — from an intimate birthday to a full wedding celebration.

  • Made fresh, to order, never mass-produced
  • One team for baking, catering and full event styling
  • Every detail — from flavour to presentation — customised to you
